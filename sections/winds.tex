\section{Winds}



What level has the most RNG out of them all? Mountain. \\
What is the cause of this? The winds.\\
\\
\subsection*{How do the winds work?}
There are 12 different winds coded into the game, Each of these winds have different strengths, off times, peak times, sounds and affect your scarf in varying ways.\\
\\

\subsubsection*{What are the properties of each Wind section?}
I will refer to the order in which you meet the winds from the start of the level playing normally (i.e no mountain skip).\\
\\
There are two different winds in the fist valley. The first wind you come across is labeled \textit{WindBattle10 internally}, This wind is special as the strength of the wind is 
weak and if you do not jump, you can walk through the wind without being pushed back. Another quirk of this wind is you will not lose any length of scarf by standing in the wind.\\
\\ 
The second wind in the first valley is labeled \textit{WindBattle11 internally}, the rng \textit{between blowing winds is about 2 seconds}, If you stand in the wind, the wind 
will push you back and you will lose 3 scarf length. However if you have 16 or less scarf length you will not lose any more, however if you have 17, you will still lose 3 and go
down to 14, if you have 18 you will go down to 15, and if you had 19 you will go down to 16.\\
\\
\textit{I believe while you are walking past the heater station there is a wind pattern there. This is WindBattle12 and is permenantly blowing, however the stength is fairly
weak.}\\
\\
Going into the patrol fields from the bridge there is a wind that takes off 2 scarf length. However if you have a scarf of length 11 or below this will not affect you. 
There is also a way to avoid this wind, by hugging the wall when doing mountain skip \autocite{gadullusPFSC}. \textit{Internally this is called WindBattle7}\\
\\
The patrol fields use \textit{WindBattle10} which does not consume any scarf length on wind.\\
\\
Wind balcony uses \textit{Either WindBattle1 or wbFortressStuff1:WindBattle3} and each wind consumes a scarf length of 3, with the total amount capped at 6.\\
\\
Death march uses \textit{WindBattle5 } and each wind consumes 5 scarf length and there is no cap, so you can and will go to 0 scarf length. 
\textit{The RNG between each wind can be between 9.5 and 0.25 seconds.}\\
\\
