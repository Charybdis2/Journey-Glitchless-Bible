\subsection*{Run Setup}

Glitchless runs can be slow. Especially redrobe runs. As such, playing offline or reducing your likley hood of
running into another wayfarer mid run is essential. 

\subsubsection{PS3}

Disconnect the network: Go to Settings \textless\  Network Settings \textless\  Internet Connection Settings and select Disable or Disconnect

\subsubsection{PS4}

Go to [Settings] \textless\  [Network] and uncheck [Connect to the Internet].\\
Alternatively, unplug your Wi-Fi router or the Ethernet cable from the back of the console

\subsubsection{PS5}
Go to Settings from your home screen. Select Users and Accounts, then click on Other. 
Choose Console Sharing and Offline Play and select Enable.

\subsubsection{PC: Steam}
Click Steam (Top left) \textless\ Go offline.. Then run the game

\subsubsection{PC: Epic Games}
Menu \textless\ settings \textless\ preferences \textless\ enable offline mode
% https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xPoRA_W8o3Y 

\subsubsection{IOS}
Go to Setting on your phone, enable airplane mode\\
\\
\\
This assumes that you don't need to play online to stream your attempts, as such the only games that can be played offline 
while streaming on the device are PC. This is why a capture card is recommended, but not requried, as you can stream while 
remaining offline. \\
\\
A way to reduce the likley hood of running into another wayfarer is that first time wayfarers cannot encounter 
whiterobes.


\subsection*{Input Preference}

On Every platform you can use a controller to play, however on pc you may use Keyboard and mouse, and IOS you can use 
touch screen. There are pros and cons to every input device, however the main drawbacks from controller to Keyboard
and mouse and less aparent in glitchless as the pressure of the movement stick is not as impactful here.
