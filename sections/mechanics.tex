\section{General Mechanics}

\subsection*{Zen Jump}
Press the screensaver button, and just before your screen turns totally black hit the jump button. Press
the screensaver button again and view the result \autocite{zenJump_Wiki}.\\
An easier alternative to this is the zen drop \autocite{zenJumpDrop_Wiki} when you are sliding in sunken city if you just hit the screensaver button
without the jump, this will be slower than a zen jump but is more consistent.\\ 
This is currently used in one place Sunken city in all categories. 
\subsection*{Animation Canceling}
Walking up bolders or terrain you can cancel the slowdown of the stumble animation by chirping.

\subsection*{Gaining extra height}
You can gain extra height by walking off a ledge then jumping as aposed to just jumping \autocite{charybdis2_2022}. In 
the \href{https://youtu.be/I1JgGJzllMw}{Video} I use a little hop by running off the ledge to gain enough height 
so only two jumps are needed for the 2nd ledge.
