\section{Scarf Infomation}

Your scarf and it's length is probably the single most important mechanic in glitchless.\\
This guide will be using the values that the game uses, These are abstracted by visually 
seeing the length of your scarf. This section heavily used some findings from Gadullus' wind theory \autocite{winds_speedrun_guide} 
and my own.\\
\\
A glossary for this section:\\
When I talk about \textbf{length} I mean the total capacity of your scarf at full charge.\\
\textbf{Charge} refering to current amount of length that you use while jumping.
\subsection*{The Charge System}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Redrobe you start with a length of 4 
    \item Whiterobe you start with a length of 10
    \item Each symbol gives either +1 or +2 length, this pattern is alternating and the previous length given is remembered even after a chapter select reset.
    \item 1 Just costs 4 length, unless you are in whiterobe and your feet are touching the ground. In this case 1 jump costs 3.
    \item You cannot exceed a length of 30.
    \item Even if you only have 1 charge left, you can still jump.
    \item If you have 0 scarf and you pick up a symbol that symbol will give 4+1 (5) or 4+2 (6) depending on your cycle
\end{itemize}
To deduce what legnth the next symbol will give, Go to chapter select and pick up 1 symbol, If you can jump 4 times
from the ground after picking up the symbol the next symbol will give +1, If you cannot jump 4 times (I.E 3)
then the next symbol will give +2.

\subsection*{Losing Scarf}
There are two ways to lose scarf. Getting hit by a War Machine or winds. The winds are a bit special and will be covered
in the Mountain section.\\
War Machines take half your scarf rounded down, so if you had 30, you will have 15, or if you had 3 you will have 0.
War machines and the final wind are the only ways to get a total of 0 scarf.\\

\subsection*{How many symbols should you take?}
In All symbols and scarfless this is irrelivent as you either take none or all of them. In any\%, 
however you need to weigh the benifits of collecting symbols with the time it takes to collect them.\\
The current Any\% route collects 7 symbols (2 in chapter Select, 3 in Pink Desert, 1 in underground and 1 in tower)
These symbols are mostly on the direct route you take (Sunken City and Underground being the best examples) 
or are used in skips (Tower being the best example). The key difference with 7 compared to 8,9,10 or even 6 or below
is the amount of jumps given and the total length. At 7 you gain 1 extra jump compared to 6 or below and you're
21 in length. This will be explained in the Mountain section about winds why this is important, but the key point
is the final death march winds take 5 length off, so at 21 you need 5 winds to completly remove your scarf.
