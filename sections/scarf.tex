\section{Scarf Infomation}

Your scarf and it's length is probably the single most important mechanic in glitchless.\\
This guide will be using the values that the game uses, These are abstracted by visually 
seeing the length of your scarf. This section heavily used some findings from Gadullus' wind theory \autocite{winds_speedrun_guide} 
and my own.\\
\\
A glossary for this section:\\
When I talk about \textbf{length} I mean the total capacity of your scarf at full charge.\\
\textbf{Charge} refering to current amount of length that you use while jumping.
\subsection*{The Charge System}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Redrobe you start with a length of 4 
    \item Whiterobe you start with a length of 10
    \item Each symbol gives either +1 or +2 length, this pattern is alternating and the previous length given is remembered even after a chapter select reset.
    \item 1 Just costs 4 length, unless you are in whiterobe and your feet are touching the ground. In this case 1 jump costs 3.
    \item You cannot exceed a length of 30.
    \item Even if you only have 1 charge left, you can still jump.
\end{itemize}
To deduce what legnth the next symbol will give, Go to chapter select and pick up 1 symbol, If you can jump 4 times
from the ground after picking up the symbol the next symbol will give +1, If you cannot jump 4 times (I.E 3)
then the next symbol will give +2.
